# Accessories

3D-printable accessories for PlancH. Each one comes with ready-to-print files and a parametric
[build123d](https://github.com/gumyr/build123d) source in
[`mechanical/`](https://github.com/caccia78/PlancH/tree/main/mechanical), so you can adapt it
to your printer, your board thickness or your desk.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-desk: **[Desk stand](desk-stand.md)**

    ---

    Holds the board at 20° on the desk, with light slots for the backlight.

-   :material-magnet: **Magnetic mount** *(planned)*

    ---

    A bracket glued to the foot positions, with embedded magnets, for interchangeable bases.

-   :material-monitor: **Monitor and keyboard mounts** *(planned)*

    ---

    Clip-on holders for the monitor bezel or next to the keyboard.

</div>

Ideas for other mounts are welcome in the
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/caccia78/PlancH/issues).
